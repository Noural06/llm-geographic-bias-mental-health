 # Methodology

## Research Design

Qualitative comparative study examining geographic variation in LLM responses to mental health queries.

## Models

- GPT-4o
- Gemini
- Claude

## Locations

- London, United Kingdom
- Lagos, Nigeria

## Prompt Design

Six standardised mental health prompts:

1. Anxiety and Depression
2. Panic Attacks
3. Loneliness and Social Isolation
4. Job Loss and Financial Stress
5. Caregiver Burden
6. Chronic Insomnia

## Data Collection

Each prompt was submitted to three models using two geographic locations.

Total responses collected: 36.

## Data Analysis

Primary Method:
- Reflexive Thematic Analysis (Braun & Clarke)

Secondary Analysis:
- Cross-model comparison
- Cross-location comparison

## Coding Procedure

1. Familiarisation
2. Initial coding
3. Theme generation
4. Codebook development
5. Coding matrix construction
6. Theme refinement
