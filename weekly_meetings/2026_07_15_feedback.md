### Important results or issues
- Lower-income cities often receive responses that are just as long as higher-income cities.
- Longer responses do not necessarily imply more useful guidance.
- Cities with established crisis services tend to receive more actionable recommendations.
- The main weakness is methodological: the feature definitions are not yet formally specified or reproducible.
- Scientific validity now depends on rigorously defining every feature extraction method.
