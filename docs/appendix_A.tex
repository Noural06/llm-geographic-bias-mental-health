\section*{A.1 Scenario Battery}

Eight scenarios were constructed to span a range of acuity levels, from moderate emotional distress through to presentations consistent with an immediate risk of self-harm. Each scenario was combined with each of the 20 city/country location statements (Appendix B) to produce a single prompt, with all other text held constant. Scenario identifiers (S1--S8) correspond to those used throughout the results tables and figures.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Scenario battery: acuity level and content summary}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
ID & Acuity & Presenting concern (summary) \\
\midrule
S1 & Moderate & Persistent loneliness and social disconnection \\
S2 & Moderate & Relationship breakdown / recent breakup \\
S3 & Moderate & Caregiver strain (long-term illness of a family member) \\
S4 & Moderate--High & Panic attacks, acute anxiety symptoms \\
S5 & Moderate & Sleep disturbance and chronic stress \\
S6 & Moderate--High & Persistent hopelessness lasting several weeks \\
S7 & High & Expressed inability to continue (\emph{``I can't keep doing this''}) \\
S8 & High & Explicit hopelessness with an implied risk indicator \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Each scenario was phrased in first person and combined with a location statement of the form ``I live in [city], [country]'' or an equivalent natural variant, followed by a request for guidance. Scenario wording was held fixed across all 20 locations and all 7 models to ensure that any observed variation in response content is attributable to the stated location rather than to variation in the query itself.

\section*{A.2 Example Prompt Template}

\begin{quote}
\textit{``I live in [CITY], [COUNTRY]. [SCENARIO TEXT]. What should I do?''}
\end{quote}

The bracketed location field was populated with each of the 20 city/country pairs listed in Appendix B, and the bracketed scenario field with each of the eight scenarios summarised above, yielding the fully-crossed $20 \times 8 = 160$ prompts issued to each of the seven models (Appendix B), for a total of 1{,}120 responses.
