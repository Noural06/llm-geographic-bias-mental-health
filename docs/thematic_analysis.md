# Thematic Analysis Notes

## Theme 1: Institutional Healthcare Navigation

Formal healthcare pathways, referrals, and specialist services.

Examples:

* NHS Talking Therapies
* GP referrals
* Hospitals
* Psychiatric services

---

## Theme 2: Support Under Resource Constraints

Recommendations adapted to limited service access.

Examples:

* NGOs
* Teletherapy
* Community resources

---

## Theme 3: Behavioural Self-Management

Strategies focused on individual behaviour change.

Examples:

* Sleep hygiene
* Exercise
* Routines
* Grounding techniques

Most common in GPT-4o.

---

## Theme 4: Localised Resource Referral

Location-specific organisations and support services.

Examples:

* NHS services
* MANI
* SURPIN
* Local hospitals

Most common in Gemini.

---

## Theme 5: Professional Mental Health Referral

Recommendations to seek professional assessment and treatment.

Examples:

* GP appointments
* Psychologists
* Psychiatrists
* Specialist clinics

Most common in Claude.

---

## Theme 6: Community Integration and Belonging

Building social connections and reducing isolation.

Examples:

* Meetup groups
* Volunteering
* Religious communities
* Alumni networks

---

## Theme 7: Financial Security and Economic Resilience

Managing unemployment and financial stress.

Examples:

* Universal Credit
* Citizens Advice
* Community savings groups
* Entrepreneurship

---

## Theme 8: Caregiver Burden and Emotional Exhaustion

Emotional strain resulting from long-term caregiving responsibilities.

Examples:

* Burnout
* Respite care
* Caregiver support services

---

## Theme 9: Formal vs Informal Support Systems

Differences between institutional support and community-based support.

Examples:

London:

* Carers Assessment
* NHS pathways

Lagos:

* Family networks
* Religious communities

---

## Theme 10: Integrated Physical and Mental Health Care

Recognition of interactions between physical health and mental well-being.

Examples:

* Insomnia
* Sleep clinics
* CBT-I
* Physical health screening
* Mental health assessment

---

## Preliminary Interpretation

Thematic analysis indicates that geographic context influences both healthcare recommendations and broader assumptions regarding social support, economic resilience, caregiving, and wellbeing.

Model-specific patterns remained stable throughout the pilot dataset.
