Study design
20 cities Low · Lower-Middle · Upper-Middle · High income. One city varies; everything else fixed.
8 scenarios Hopelessness · caregiver exhaustion · insomnia · panic · loneliness · more. Identical English wording.
7 models GPT-OSS · Qwen3 · Gemma4 · Llama4 · Llama3.3 · Nemotron · Mistral. One response per cell.
Rule-based NLP Regex + vocabularies. Every decision traceable. No ML weights. Think-block removal first.
Validation gate 160 fresh hold-out responses + 40 repeat-coded responses. Separate 100-response H3 sample.
Pre-registered models Linear mixed-effects (H1, H2). Firth logistic (H3). Validation determines confirmatory status.

Methodological contribution
Automated rules can agree on the wrong answer — in the wrong direction
The original localisation measure rewarded contact omission in under-served cities, producing the opposite coefficient to the corrected measure. Two automated rule sets agreed at κ > 0.93 without detecting the flaw. A fresh hold-out then showed that the replacement surface-localisation rule had specificity of only 0.038. Agreement, development-set performance and high F₁ are not substitutes for independent, class-sensitive validation.
Robustness
Exploratory stability · leave one model out
H1 range: +0.558 to +0.708. H2 range: +0.299 to +0.407. Signs persist under every exclusion, but robustness of a coefficient cannot rescue an outcome that failed measurement validation.
City-level sensitivity · n = 20
Refitting on 20 city means gives identical point estimates under the balanced design. The H1 city-mean gradient is monotone (Spearman ρ = 0.848), but remains exploratory because its underlying measure did not pass validation.
Limitations & future work
  • Measurement validation failed. On the fresh 160-response hold-out, H1/H2 measures missed pre-specified criteria; their statistical patterns are exploratory.
  • Single coder. No second human coder was available. Intra-rater κ on 40 repeat-coded responses was below 0.70 for every measure (actionability κ = 0.134; verified localisation κ = 0.565).
  • No-location control. Cannot separate training-data-density effects from location-conditioned output changes. One additional API run would enable this.
  • Geographic scope. 20 cities, one per country. Effective n = 20 for geographic contrasts. Income, region, infrastructure and culture are correlated and cannot be cleanly separated at this scale.
  • English only. Users querying in local languages may face wider disparities than observed here.
  • Contact uncertainty remains. The audit was completed, but 307 records remained unresolved after source checking; these are reported as unknown, not fabricated.
