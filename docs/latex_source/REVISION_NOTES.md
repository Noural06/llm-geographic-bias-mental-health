# Revision notes

This version responds directly to the supervisory feedback and tightens the scientific claims.

## What changed

- The main story is now stated in plain language: location-conditioned differences are observed in the 20-city sample, but income is not treated as a causal mechanism.
- Actionability is decomposed into its components. The reported income gradient is largely driven by crisis-contact inclusion and is attenuated after documented service availability is added to the model.
- Surface localisation is separated from verified-contact localisation. The latter is clearly labelled as an unvalidated sensitivity measure.
- Religious recommendations are described as location-conditioned cultural framing, not stereotyping.
- Crisis contacts are classified as verified real, general emergency, visibly suspicious or unresolved. “Unverifiable” is not treated as “fabricated”.
- The inconsistent count of unresolved contacts was corrected to 307 throughout.
- The 20-city effective sample size, geographic confounding, one-generation design and single-coder limitation are stated explicitly.
- The frozen revised actionability and localisation rules were evaluated on a fresh blinded 160-response hold-out. Neither met the pre-specified gate, so RQ1 and RQ2 are exploratory.
- The initial repeat-coding exercise is reported as coding-instrument drift because the scale and rule interpretations changed between occasions; it is not presented as acceptable intra-rater reliability.
- The notebook now exports a run manifest, city-level contextual table and validation-disagreement audit.

## What remains to be done before publication

1. Complete a frozen-codebook repeat assessment and add a second independent human coder if feasible.
2. Complete source-based adjudication for every unresolved crisis contact.
3. Add a small repeated-generation subset to estimate within-cell output variability.

No new numerical results were invented in this revision. Where the attached data were insufficient to rerun an analysis, the text was made more conservative and the notebook was prepared to produce the required audit outputs when the source files are supplied.
