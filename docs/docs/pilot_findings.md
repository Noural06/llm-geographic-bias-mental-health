# Pilot Dataset Summary
##### 36 responses collected.
##### 6 prompts:
- Anxiety and Depression
- Panic Attacks
- Loneliness
- Job Loss
- Caregiver Burden
- Insomnia
  ##### 2 locations:
- London
- Lagos
##### 3 models:
- GPT-4o
- Gemini
- Claude
##### 10 preliminary themes identified.

## Key Observation:
All models adapted their responses to the geographic context. However, the degree of localisation and support style varied significantly between models.
###### Gemini:
Most localised.
###### GPT-4o:
Most behavioural and strategic.
###### Claude:
Most balanced and referral-oriented.
### Preliminary Conclusion:
Geographic context influences not only healthcare referrals but also assumptions about social support, economic resilience, caregiving responsibilities, and access to mental health services.
