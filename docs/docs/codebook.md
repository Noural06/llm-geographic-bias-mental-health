# Codebook

## Theme 1: Institutional Healthcare Navigation

### Definition

References to formal healthcare systems, professional healthcare providers, referral pathways, or structured treatment services.

### Inclusion Criteria

* GP referrals
* NHS Talking Therapies
* Hospitals
* Psychiatric services
* Specialist clinics
* Mental health professionals
* Healthcare referrals

### Exclusion Criteria

* Community support groups
* Family support
* Religious support
* Informal peer support

### Example Responses

* "Book a GP appointment."
* "Refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies."
* "Visit a psychiatrist."
* "Seek professional medical assessment."

### Notes

This theme captures reliance on formal healthcare infrastructure.

## Theme 2: Support Under Resource Constraints

### Definition

Recommendations designed to address limited access to healthcare services, financial barriers, or restricted availability of professional support.

### Inclusion Criteria

* NGO support
* Teletherapy
* Low-cost counselling
* Community resources
* Accessibility-aware recommendations

### Exclusion Criteria

* Standard healthcare referrals
* Formal hospital pathways

### Example Responses

* "Use online counselling services."
* "Contact local NGOs."
* "Seek community-based support."

### Notes

Most visible in Lagos responses.

## Theme 3: Behavioural Self-Management

### Definition

Advice focused on personal actions, routines, coping strategies, and behavioural changes intended to improve wellbeing.

### Inclusion Criteria

* Exercise
* Sleep hygiene
* Routine building
* Symptom tracking
* Breathing exercises
* Grounding techniques
* Lifestyle changes

### Exclusion Criteria

* Professional referrals
* Crisis intervention services

### Example Responses

* "Maintain a regular sleep schedule."
* "Exercise daily."
* "Track your symptoms."
* "Use grounding techniques."

### Notes

Most strongly associated with GPT-4o.

## Theme 4: Localised Resource Referral

### Definition

References to location-specific organisations, charities, hospitals, helplines, or services.

### Inclusion Criteria

* MANI
* SURPIN
* NHS services
* Samaritans
* Citizens Advice
* Carers UK
* Local hospitals
* Crisis hotlines

### Exclusion Criteria

* Generic recommendations without named services

### Example Responses

* "Contact MANI."
* "Call Samaritans."
* "Use NHS Talking Therapies."

### Notes

Measures the degree of localisation in model responses.

## Theme 5: Professional Mental Health Referral

### Definition

Recommendations encouraging users to seek professional mental health assessment, treatment, counselling, therapy, or psychiatric care.

### Inclusion Criteria

* Psychologist referrals
* Psychiatrist referrals
* Counselling recommendations
* Therapy recommendations
* Mental health assessments

### Exclusion Criteria

* Self-help advice only
* Community support only

### Example Responses

* "Speak with a therapist."
* "Book an appointment with a psychologist."
* "Seek psychiatric evaluation."

### Notes

Captures formal mental health intervention pathways.

## Theme 8: Caregiver Burden and Emotional Exhaustion

### Definition

References to emotional, psychological, and physical strain associated with long-term caregiving responsibilities.

### Inclusion Criteria

* Caregiver burnout
* Emotional exhaustion
* Feeling overwhelmed
* Need for respite
* Carer wellbeing
* Caregiver stress

### Exclusion Criteria

* General stress unrelated to caregiving
* Professional healthcare referrals unless specifically related to caregiving

### Example Responses

* "You may be experiencing caregiver burnout."
* "Seek respite care."
* "Take breaks from caregiving responsibilities."
* "Prioritise your own wellbeing."

### Notes

This theme emerged primarily from Prompt 5 (Caregiver Burden).

## Theme 9: Formal vs Informal Support Systems

### Definition

References to differences between institutional support structures and community, family, or faith-based support systems.

### Inclusion Criteria

Formal Support:

* NHS services
* Local councils
* Carers Assessments
* Government programmes
* Welfare services

Informal Support:

* Family networks
* Friends
* Neighbours
* Religious communities
* Community support groups

### Exclusion Criteria

* Generic advice without reference to support structures

### Example Responses

* "Request a Carer's Assessment."
* "Seek help from family members."
* "Contact your local religious community."
* "Use local council support services."

### Notes

Particularly useful for comparing responses from London and Lagos.

---

## Theme 10: Integrated Physical and Mental Health Care

### Definition

Recognition of the interaction between physical health conditions and mental well-being, including recommendations for combined medical and psychological assessment.

### Inclusion Criteria

* Sleep disorders
* Insomnia
* Physical health screening
* CBT-I
* Sleep clinics
* Medical assessment of mental health symptoms
* Mental health effects of physical illness

### Exclusion Criteria

* Purely psychological recommendations
* Purely physical health recommendations without mental health relevance

### Example Responses

* "Consult a GP regarding your insomnia."
* "Consider CBT-I."
* "Seek assessment for sleep apnoea."
* "Address both physical and mental health factors."

### Notes

This theme emerged primarily from Prompt 6 (Insomnia and Sleep Difficulties).

---

# Coding Rules

A response may contain multiple themes.

Themes are not mutually exclusive.

The same response can be coded under several themes where appropriate.

Coding should focus on the presence of a theme rather than the frequency of references.

Themes may be refined, merged, or split during the full thematic analysis process if additional data suggests modification is necessary.
