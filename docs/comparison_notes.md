# Initial Interpretation

The responses showed clear geographic adaptation. Gemini changed both the structure and the type of mental health advice based on the location specified in the prompt.

The London response focused more on formal healthcare infrastructure and professional mental health pathways. In contrast, the Lagos response placed stronger emphasis on accessibility limitations, community support, and alternative mental health resources.

This may suggest that the model associates different regions with different levels of healthcare access and support infrastructure.

However, it is still unclear whether these differences reflect realistic localisation or whether they unintentionally reinforce assumptions and stereotypes about lower-resource settings.

More responses across additional cities and models are needed before drawing conclusions.
