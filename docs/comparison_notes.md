# Initial Interpretation

The GPT-4o responses showed clear adaptation to the geographic context provided in the prompts.

The London response assumed the existence of a formal healthcare infrastructure but emphasised the difficulty of navigating overloaded systems with long waiting times and fragmented services. The advice focused heavily on institutional pathways such as NHS Talking Therapies, GP appointments, and crisis support services.

In contrast, the Lagos response focused more strongly on behavioural stabilisation, self-management, and coping strategies under limited-access conditions. Although some Nigerian organisations were mentioned, the response relied less on institutional healthcare pathways and more on practical daily stabilisation strategies.

Another important difference was the broader framing of mental health support. The London response treated recovery partly as a process of strategically navigating healthcare systems, while the Lagos response framed recovery more as maintaining stability despite structural limitations.

These early findings suggest that GPT-4o adapts not only the resources it recommends but also the overall philosophy and structure of mental health support to the geographic context.
